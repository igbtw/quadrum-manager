Dashboard

Your daily overview: sales and revenue for today, receivables and overdue balances, low-stock alerts, and the latest sales activity.

Open Dashboard
2

Sales

The sales history lists recent sales with their status (Completed, Pending, or Cancelled), totals, and customer. Use it to review what was finalized and when.

View Sales
3

New Sale

The point of sale: pick products by category, set quantities, optionally choose the customer, select the payment method, then finalize. Finalizing records the sale and deducts stock; an On Account sale with a customer also creates an account receivable.

Start a Sale
4

Products and Inventory

Products holds the catalog (price, cost, minimum stock). Inventory shows current stock and the manual adjustment form: enter a positive quantity to add stock or a negative quantity to remove it, with a reason. Every change is logged as a stock movement.

Products Inventory
5

People and Finance

People stores students, teachers, staff, and visitors used as sale customers. Finance tracks outstanding accounts and registers payments, updating each receivable's status automatically. Reports summarizes operational activity.

People Finance
