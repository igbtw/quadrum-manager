People are the customer records used by sales and finance: students, teachers, staff, and visitors. The People page lists and filters them.

Record types

  • Student, Teacher, Staff, and Visitor — the record type is shown as a badge in the listing and in payment details.

Record fields

FieldShown asNotes
NameNameUsed for matching in listings and search.
TypeType badgeStudent, Teacher, Staff, or Visitor.
Record / DocRegistrationA short identifier (for example, a student number) usable for lookup.
StatusActive / Inactive badgeInactive records stay in history but are excluded from the active listing.
BalanceBalance columnOutstanding amount from open receivables. Negative values (red) mean the person owes money.

Filtering and lookup

  • The listing header has a search box that filters by name or registration; the URL parameter is preserved, so links can open a filtered listing directly.
  • All types and All statuses selects narrow the visible rows by person type and active status.
  • Each row has a copy action that puts the registration value on the clipboard.

Searching for people from anywhere

  • Press Ctrl+K and type a name or registration number — matching people appear under the People category.
  • Use the person: prefix to search people only (for example, person:maria or person:123456), or switch to the People tab.
  • Selecting a person opens the People listing already filtered to that name.
  • Search results show only the name and record details — email and phone numbers are never returned by search.

See Search and Advanced Queries for the full search syntax.
