Products form the catalog that feeds the point of sale. The Products page manages the catalog; the Inventory page shows stock and records adjustments.

Product fields

FieldMeaning
nameThe product name shown in the catalog and sale screens.
codeA unique short code used for lookup (for example, COX-01).
categoryThe grouping used to filter the catalog and the point-of-sale product picker.
priceSale price (R$, two decimals). The POS always uses this stored price.
cost_priceOptional acquisition cost for reference.
stock_quantityCurrent stock. Never allowed to be negative.
minimum_stockAlert threshold used by the low-stock indicator.
activeInactive products stay in history but are hidden from sale selection.

Stock status indicators

  • Normal — stock is above the minimum.
  • Low — stock is above zero but at or below the minimum stock threshold.
  • Out of Stock — stock is exactly zero; the product cannot be sold.
  • Inactive — the product is deactivated; its stock is not part of the active counts.

Adjusting stock

  1. Open Inventory and use the stock adjustment form.
  2. Pick the product, enter the quantity — positive to add stock, negative to remove — and a reason.
  3. Submit. The new stock level is saved together with a stock movement recording the signed quantity, the reason, and the responsible user.
  • Stock never goes negative: an adjustment that would remove more than the available quantity is rejected with an error message, and nothing is saved.
  • Every change is an append-only audit trail; sales produce negative movements, cancellations produce returns, and manual adjustments are recorded as adjustments.
  • Zero-quantity adjustments are rejected.

Movement types, locking behavior, and the negative-stock constraint are detailed in docs/business-flows.md and docs/data-model.md (repository files, not served by the application).
