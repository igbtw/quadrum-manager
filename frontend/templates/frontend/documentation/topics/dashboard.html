The Dashboard is the landing page after sign-in. It summarizes the current day and recent activity; the Reports page covers longer periods.

Overview cards

  • Sales today — the number of Completed sales created today.
  • Revenue today — the sum of the totals of those Completed sales, shown in R$ with two decimals.
  • Open receivables — the sum of the original amounts of receivables currently in Pending, Partial, or Overdue state (not the remaining balance after payments).
  • Low stock — the number of active products whose stock is at or below their minimum stock, but still above zero.

Alerts and activity

  • Low-stock list — active products with stock at or below the minimum threshold, with their current quantities.
  • Out-of-stock count — active products with zero stock; these cannot be sold until restocked.
  • Recent sales — the five most recent sales with their code, total, and status. The full history is on the Sales page.

Where the numbers come from

  • All dashboard figures are computed from the database at page load; there is no caching layer to refresh.
  • “Today” uses the server's current date and time zone.
  • Because the open-receivables card uses original amounts, it can exceed the money actually still owed once payments are registered. The remaining balance per receivable is visible on Finance.

The detailed aggregation rules are documented for developers in docs/business-flows.md (repository file, not served by the application).
