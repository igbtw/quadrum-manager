The application keeps a compact data model: nine entities covering the catalog, sales, stock auditing, receivables, and onboarding state. This page is a quick map; the schema, constraints, and migration guidance live in docs/data-model.md.

Entities

ModelPurpose
CategoryGroups products; referenced with PROTECT so a category in use cannot be deleted.
ProductName, code, category, price, cost, stock, minimum stock, and active flag.
PersonCustomer registry with type, record/doc, contact fields, and active flag.
SaleSale header: code, status, payment method, totals, customer, responsible user, and idempotency key.
SaleItemOne line of a sale with the captured unit price at sale time.
StockMovementAppend-only audit log of every stock change (sale, return, adjustment), with optional sale and user links.
AccountReceivableAmount owed by a person for an On Account sale.
PaymentA payment applied to a receivable.
OnboardingProgressFirst-run guide state per account, including the resume step.

Business rules the application enforces

  • Stock is never allowed to go negative — a database constraint backs the service-level checks.
  • Payments never exceed the outstanding balance, and cancelled receivables refuse new payments at multiple layers.
  • A completed sale keeps its captured prices; later catalog changes do not rewrite its history.
  • Product and person records referenced by sales are protected from deletion (PROTECT); the sale keeps a nullable link if a customer is removed (SET_NULL).
  • Monetary values keep two decimal places.
  • Stock movement history is append-only, so stock changes can always be reconstructed.
