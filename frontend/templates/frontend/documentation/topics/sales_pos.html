Sales are recorded at the point of sale and listed in the Sales history. Each sale stores its line items, totals, payment method, customer (when attached), and the responsible user.

Recording a sale

  1. Open New Sale and pick products by category with the quantity controls.
  2. Review the cart total. Prices and totals are always calculated from the stored product price.
  3. Choose the payment method and select Finalize Sale.
  4. The sale is recorded as Completed, stock is deducted from each product, and a stock movement is written per item.

Payment methods

  • Cash, Debit, Credit, and PIX close the sale immediately.
  • On Account records the sale and, when the sale has a customer attached, creates an account receivable for that person — managed on Finance. An On Account sale without a customer completes but creates no receivable.

Sale states

  • Pending — created but not finalized (not the normal outcome of the checkout flow).
  • Completed — finalized; stock was deducted and the total recorded. Completed sales keep their history.
  • Cancelled — a cancelled sale remains visible in history; when applicable, stock is restored and its receivable is closed. See docs/business-flows.md for the exact cancellation restrictions (for example, a receivable that already has payments blocks cancellation).

Validation and safe retries

  • Quantities must be positive whole numbers; invalid items are rejected before anything is written.
  • A sale that would consume more stock than available is rejected and nothing is saved — stock never goes negative.
  • Finalizing uses an idempotency key: if the same submission is retried (for example after a network hiccup), the existing sale is returned instead of creating a duplicate. Reusing a key with different contents is rejected with a conflict.

The full request/response contract, including every error code, is on the API Reference page and in docs/api-reference.md.
