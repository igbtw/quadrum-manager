Finance tracks money owed by people through accounts receivable and the payments applied against them. Everything happens on the Finance page.

Where receivables come from

  • A receivable is created when a sale is finalized as On Account with a customer attached. Its original amount is the sale total and its due date is the completion date.
  • An On Account sale without a customer completes but creates no receivable.
  • Each receivable belongs to one person and lists its original amount, remaining balance, due date, status, and registered payments.

Receivable statuses

StatusMeaning
PendingCreated and awaiting payment.
PartialSome payments registered; a balance remains.
PaidFully settled.
OverduePast the due date with a balance remaining.
CancelledClosed — no further payments are accepted.

Statuses are recalculated when payments are registered or a sale is cancelled; there is no background job that moves records to Overdue on its own, so a stored status reflects the last recalculation.

Registering payments

  1. Open Finance and pick the receivable.
  2. Enter the amount and the method: Cash, Debit, Credit, PIX, or Transfer.
  3. Optionally add notes, then submit. The balance and status update together.
  • A payment cannot exceed the remaining balance and cannot be zero or negative.
  • Payments on cancelled receivables are refused.

Cancellation rules

  • Cancelling a sale marks its receivable as Cancelled (the record is never deleted) and it disappears from the payment selector.
  • A sale whose receivable already has registered payments cannot be cancelled — the payments are a hard block.
  • A cancelled receivable is terminal: it is never reopened to Pending or Partial.

Monetary values are stored with two decimal places and displayed in R$. The exact transactional rules and lock ordering are documented in docs/business-flows.md (repository file, not served by the application).
