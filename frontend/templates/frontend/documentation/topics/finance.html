{% blocktrans trimmed %}Finance tracks money owed by people through accounts receivable and the payments applied against them. Everything happens on the Finance page.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}Where receivables come from{% endblocktrans %}

  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}A receivable is created when a sale is finalized as On Account with a customer attached. Its original amount is the sale total and its due date is the completion date.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}An On Account sale without a customer completes but creates no receivable.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}Each receivable belongs to one person and lists its original amount, remaining balance, due date, status, and registered payments.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}Receivable statuses{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}Status{% endblocktrans %}{% blocktrans trimmed %}Meaning{% endblocktrans %}
{% blocktrans trimmed %}Pending{% endblocktrans %}{% blocktrans trimmed %}Created and awaiting payment.{% endblocktrans %}
{% blocktrans trimmed %}Partial{% endblocktrans %}{% blocktrans trimmed %}Some payments registered; a balance remains.{% endblocktrans %}
{% blocktrans trimmed %}Paid{% endblocktrans %}{% blocktrans trimmed %}Fully settled.{% endblocktrans %}
{% blocktrans trimmed %}Overdue{% endblocktrans %}{% blocktrans trimmed %}Past the due date with a balance remaining.{% endblocktrans %}
{% blocktrans trimmed %}Cancelled{% endblocktrans %}{% blocktrans trimmed %}Closed — no further payments are accepted.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}Statuses are recalculated when payments are registered or a sale is cancelled; there is no background job that moves records to Overdue on its own, so a stored status reflects the last recalculation.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}Registering payments{% endblocktrans %}

    {% url 'frontend:finance' as _u1 %}
  1. {% blocktrans trimmed %}Open Finance and pick the receivable.{% endblocktrans %}
  2. {% blocktrans trimmed %}Enter the amount and the method: Cash, Debit, Credit, PIX, or Transfer.{% endblocktrans %}
  3. {% blocktrans trimmed %}Optionally add notes, then submit. The balance and status update together.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}A payment cannot exceed the remaining balance and cannot be zero or negative.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}Payments on cancelled receivables are refused.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}Cancellation rules{% endblocktrans %}

  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}Cancelling a sale marks its receivable as Cancelled (the record is never deleted) and it disappears from the payment selector.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}A sale whose receivable already has registered payments cannot be cancelled — the payments are a hard block.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}A cancelled receivable is terminal: it is never reopened to Pending or Partial.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}Monetary values are stored with two decimal places and displayed in R$. The exact transactional rules and lock ordering are documented in docs/business-flows.md (repository file, not served by the application).{% endblocktrans %}
