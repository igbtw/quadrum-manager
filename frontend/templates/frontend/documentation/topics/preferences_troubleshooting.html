Interface preferences are stored in this browser only (localStorage key quadrum.preferences.v1) and apply immediately. They never sync across devices or accounts.

Available preferences

PreferenceOptions
ThemeSystem, Light, Dark.
Accent colorDefault, Blue, Violet, Emerald — tints buttons, links, focus rings, and selected items.
Interface densityComfortable or Compact spacing in panels, tables, and forms.
Corner styleSquare (default) or Rounded.
Desktop sidebarExpanded or Icon-only.
Text sizeSmall, Default, Large.
MotionSystem or Reduced animation.
High contrastStrengthen text, borders, and focus indicators.
Confirm destructive actionsAsk before deletes or removals that cannot be undone.

Open the Preferences dialog from the header or search inside it; clearing the search shows all settings again. There is no “save” button — changes apply immediately. A stored preference is not the cause of data disappearing; clearing browser storage resets the interface to defaults.

Troubleshooting

  • Signed out unexpectedly? Sessions expire after a period of inactivity. Sign in again; saved data is not affected.
  • Stock adjustment rejected? Stock cannot go negative. Reduce the quantity to remove or restock first.
  • Payment rejected? The amount exceeds the remaining balance, or the receivable is cancelled. Overpayment is blocked at the form and service layers.
  • Can't cancel a sale? A sale whose receivable already has registered payments cannot be cancelled, and only Completed sales can be cancelled.
  • Product missing from the sale picker? Inactive products are hidden from selection but remain in history.
  • Person missing from the listing? The listing shows active records; a record that was deactivated stays in history but is not listed.
  • Want the tour again? Use Show getting started guide on the Documentation index.
  • Something else? The Help control in the header always returns to the documentation index.
